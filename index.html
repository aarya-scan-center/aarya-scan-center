Our services

Imaging services,
explained simply.

Explore the ultrasound and Doppler services offered at Aarya Scan Center. Your doctor can help you decide which test is appropriate for you.

General Ultrasound (USG)

Uses sound waves to create images of organs and soft tissues in areas such as the abdomen, pelvis, chest, neck, joints and other small parts.

Organ & soft tissue imaging

Breast Ultrasound

Creates images of breast tissue and may help assess a lump, swelling or other area of concern when recommended by a clinician.

Breast tissue assessment

Eye B-Scan

An ultrasound examination that helps clinicians view structures inside the eye, especially when the view through the front of the eye is limited.

Ocular imaging

Arterial & Venous Doppler

Assesses blood flow through selected arteries and veins. It may help evaluate circulation and check for certain blood-flow problems.

Blood flow assessment

Carotid & Renal Doppler

Evaluates blood flow in the neck arteries supplying the brain or in blood vessels supplying the kidneys, when clinically indicated.

Vascular assessment

Early Pregnancy Scan

May help confirm the location and development of an early pregnancy and estimate gestational age, based on the stage and clinical advice.

Early pregnancy assessment

NT Scan

A first-trimester ultrasound measurement used as part of prenatal screening. It should be interpreted with other screening information by a qualified clinician.

Prenatal screening

Routine Obstetric Scan

Checks aspects of the baby's growth, position, heartbeat and pregnancy progress according to the stage of pregnancy and clinician's advice.

Pregnancy monitoring

Biophysical Profile (BPP)

Combines ultrasound observations, and sometimes a fetal heart-rate test, to assess selected signs of the baby's well-being before birth.

Fetal well-being

Growth Scan + Doppler

Estimates the baby's size and growth, while Doppler may assess blood flow in selected vessels when advised by the clinician.

Growth & circulation

Anomaly Scan

A detailed mid-pregnancy ultrasound that examines the baby's developing anatomy. No scan can identify every condition.

Fetal anatomy assessment

Transvaginal Scan (TVS)

Uses a specially designed ultrasound probe to obtain detailed images of the uterus, ovaries and nearby pelvic structures when appropriate.

Pelvic imaging

Neurosonogram

An ultrasound examination of selected structures of the brain, commonly used in newborns when clinically indicated.

Brain imaging

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Find answers to questions about our services and visit preparation.

What should I bring to my appointment?

Please bring your doctor's referral, photo ID, and any previous medical reports related to your scan.

How long does a scan take?

Most scans take 15-30 minutes depending on the type of examination. Your appointment time may include registration and consultation.

When will I get my results?

Results are typically ready within 24 hours. Your referring doctor will receive your report to discuss the findings with you.
